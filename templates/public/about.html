About Diploma Study Hub

Empowering Karnataka Diploma Students with Accessible and Organised Learning Resources.

Diploma Study Hub is an educational platform created to bring Karnataka DTE diploma notes, previous year question papers, lab manuals, syllabus copies, and exam preparation materials together in one structured, easy-to-browse place.

{% include "components/section_header.html" with title="Our mission & vision" subtitle="Why Diploma Study Hub exists and where we are heading." %}

Our Mission

Our mission is to make diploma education resources easily accessible to every Karnataka diploma student. By organising materials clearly by scheme, branch, semester, and subject, we aim to simplify exam preparation and reduce the time students spend searching across scattered links and groups.

Our Vision

Our vision is to build a trusted, centralised educational platform for Karnataka diploma students where they can find academic resources easily—from semester-one fundamentals to final-year subjects—in a clean, reliable, student-first environment.

Who Can Use Our Platform?

Diploma Study Hub is intended for Karnataka Diploma students across different engineering branches and semesters (Semesters 1 through 6).

Whether you are preparing for internal assessments, laboratory practicals, or semester-end DTE examinations, you can browse available subjects and download verified materials without creating an account.

Supported Schemes

The platform is structured around Karnataka DTE diploma schemes. Study materials and branch coverage are being added progressively—not every scheme or branch has complete resources yet.
